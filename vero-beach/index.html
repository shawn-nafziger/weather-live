3 · The record book

Vero Beach is setting record highs far faster than chance

If Vero Beach's climate weren't changing, every year would have the same chance of holding a daily record. Since 2000, Vero Beach has set 82 standing record lows, fewer than the 117 that chance alone would give, and 184 record highs, far more than the 117 that chance alone would give. The 2020s hold the most record highs of any decade (76). Vero Beach's all-time high is 102°F, on June 22, 2009.

4 · How it's changed

Vero Beach's nights have warmed since 1986. Its days have warmed.

Across 81 complete years: Vero Beach's average low shows no clear change: +0.14°F per decade, a range (−0.01 to +0.29) that includes zero. It's close, but the range still includes zero. Vero Beach's average high has risen +0.32°F per decade (95% range +0.24 to +0.39), about +2.6°F since 1943. The last 40 years tell a different story: since 1986, lows have risen +0.40°F per decade (95% range +0.06 to +0.75) and highs have risen +0.37°F per decade (95% range +0.15 to +0.59). The full record joins different observing sites over the years, and a move (for example, from the city to a cooler airport) can hide or exaggerate a long-run trend, so the recent decades are the clearer guide here.

How to read these
  • Trend: a straight line through every complete year. "Per decade" is how much it rises or falls every 10 years.
  • 95% range: how far the trend could plausibly be off, given how much years bounce around. If the whole range is above zero, the warming is real, not luck.
  • Signal, at the edge, noise: signal means the range clears zero; at the edge means it's at least 80% of the way there; noise means it doesn't come close.

5 · Rain and snow

Rain: no clear long-term change

Vero Beach's yearly precipitation shows no clear long-term change (+0.01 inches per decade, a range that includes zero). The 1991–2020 normal is 53.4 inches a year. The wettest year was 1982 (81.74 in); the driest, 1961 (32.70 in). Vero Beach almost never sees snow (the 1991–2020 average is 0.0 inches a winter), so there's no snowfall chart.
