3 · The record book

Fort Lauderdale has nearly stopped setting record lows

If Fort Lauderdale's climate weren't changing, every year would have the same chance of holding a daily record. Since 2000, Fort Lauderdale has set 9 standing record lows, far fewer than the 89 that chance alone would give, and 71 record highs, close to the 88 that chance alone would give. The 1920s hold the most record highs of any decade (72). Fort Lauderdale's all-time high is 100°F, on August 4, 1944.

4 · How it's changed

Fort Lauderdale's nights have warmed. Its days have warmed since 1984.

Across 94 complete years: Fort Lauderdale's average low has risen +0.60°F per decade (95% range +0.52 to +0.68), about +6.6°F since 1914. Fort Lauderdale's average high shows no clear change: −0.05°F per decade, a range (−0.11 to +0.02) that includes zero. The last 40 years tell a different story: since 1984, lows have risen +0.97°F per decade (95% range +0.74 to +1.20) and highs have risen +0.30°F per decade (95% range +0.12 to +0.48). The full record joins different observing sites over the years, and a move (for example, from the city to a cooler airport) can hide or exaggerate a long-run trend, so the recent decades are the clearer guide here.

How to read these
  • Trend: a straight line through every complete year. "Per decade" is how much it rises or falls every 10 years.
  • 95% range: how far the trend could plausibly be off, given how much years bounce around. If the whole range is above zero, the warming is real, not luck.
  • Signal, at the edge, noise: signal means the range clears zero; at the edge means it's at least 80% of the way there; noise means it doesn't come close.

5 · Rain and snow

Rain: no clear long-term change

Fort Lauderdale's yearly precipitation shows no clear long-term change (+0.13 inches per decade, a range that includes zero). The 1991–2020 normal is 62.8 inches a year. The wettest year was 2023 (113.92 in); the driest, 1961 (35.54 in). Fort Lauderdale almost never sees snow (the 1991–2020 average is 0.0 inches a winter), so there's no snowfall chart.
