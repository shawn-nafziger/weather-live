3 · The record book

Brainerd has nearly stopped setting record lows

If Brainerd's climate weren't changing, every year would have the same chance of holding a daily record. Since 2000, Brainerd has set 26 standing record lows, far fewer than the 84 that chance alone would give, and 114 record highs, more than the 86 that chance alone would give. The 1930s hold the most record highs of any decade (54). Brainerd's all-time high is 106°F, on July 10, 1936.

4 · How it's changed

Brainerd has warmed, days and nights alike

Across 95 complete years: Brainerd's average low has risen +0.24°F per decade (95% range +0.10 to +0.38), about +3.0°F since 1899. Brainerd's average high has risen +0.18°F per decade (95% range +0.07 to +0.30), about +2.3°F since 1899.

How to read these
  • Trend: a straight line through every complete year. "Per decade" is how much it rises or falls every 10 years.
  • 95% range: how far the trend could plausibly be off, given how much years bounce around. If the whole range is above zero, the warming is real, not luck.
  • Signal, at the edge, noise: signal means the range clears zero; at the edge means it's at least 80% of the way there; noise means it doesn't come close.

5 · Rain and snow

Rain and snow: no clear long-term change

Brainerd's yearly precipitation shows no clear long-term change (+0.18 inches per decade, a range that includes zero). The 1991–2020 normal is 25.9 inches a year. The wettest year was 1986 (37.45 in); the driest, 2006 (12.68 in).
