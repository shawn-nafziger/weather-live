3 · The record book

Meridian is setting record highs far faster than chance

If Meridian's climate weren't changing, every year would have the same chance of holding a daily record. Since 2000, Meridian has set 42 standing record lows, fewer than the 71 that chance alone would give, and 112 record highs, far more than the 71 that chance alone would give. The 1950s hold the most record highs of any decade (61). Meridian's all-time high is 107°F, on July 14, 1980.

4 · How it's changed

Meridian's nights cooled over the long run but have warmed since 1985. Its days have warmed.

Across 135 complete years: Meridian's average low has fallen −0.08°F per decade (95% range −0.15 to −0.01), about −1.1°F since 1890. Meridian's average high has risen +0.25°F per decade (95% range +0.19 to +0.31), about +3.3°F since 1890. The last 40 years tell a different story: since 1985, lows have risen +0.57°F per decade (95% range +0.17 to +0.98) and highs have risen +0.44°F per decade (95% range +0.05 to +0.83). The full record joins different observing sites over the years, and a move (for example, from the city to a cooler airport) can hide or exaggerate a long-run trend, so the recent decades are the clearer guide here.

How to read these
  • Trend: a straight line through every complete year. "Per decade" is how much it rises or falls every 10 years.
  • 95% range: how far the trend could plausibly be off, given how much years bounce around. If the whole range is above zero, the warming is real, not luck.
  • Signal, at the edge, noise: signal means the range clears zero; at the edge means it's at least 80% of the way there; noise means it doesn't come close.

5 · Rain and snow

Meridian has gotten wetter

Meridian's yearly precipitation has risen +0.47 inches per decade (95% range +0.04 to +0.89), about +6.3 inches since 1890. The 1991–2020 normal is 57.0 inches a year. The wettest year was 1973 (79.03 in); the driest, 1914 (34.92 in). Meridian almost never sees snow (the 1991–2020 average is 0.8 inches a winter), so there's no snowfall chart.
