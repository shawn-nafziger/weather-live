3 · The record book

Dallas (Love Field) has nearly stopped setting record lows

If Dallas (Love Field)'s climate weren't changing, every year would have the same chance of holding a daily record. Since 2000, Dallas (Love Field) has set 22 standing record lows, far fewer than the 87 that chance alone would give, and 121 record highs, more than the 87 that chance alone would give. The 1950s hold the most record highs of any decade (58). Dallas (Love Field)'s all-time high is 112°F, on June 26, 1980.

4 · How it's changed

Dallas (Love Field) has warmed, days and nights alike

Across 111 complete years: Dallas (Love Field)'s average low has risen +0.30°F per decade (95% range +0.23 to +0.37), about +3.3°F since 1914. Dallas (Love Field)'s average high has risen +0.29°F per decade (95% range +0.21 to +0.37), about +3.3°F since 1914.

How to read these
  • Trend: a straight line through every complete year. "Per decade" is how much it rises or falls every 10 years.
  • 95% range: how far the trend could plausibly be off, given how much years bounce around. If the whole range is above zero, the warming is real, not luck.
  • Signal, at the edge, noise: signal means the range clears zero; at the edge means it's at least 80% of the way there; noise means it doesn't come close.
