3 · The record book

Gulkana has nearly stopped setting record lows

If Gulkana's climate weren't changing, every year would have the same chance of holding a daily record. Since 2000, Gulkana has set 51 standing record lows, far fewer than the 111 that chance alone would give, and 135 record highs, close to the 111 that chance alone would give. The 2010s hold the most record highs of any decade (73). Gulkana's all-time high is 97°F, on July 10, 1915.

4 · How it's changed

Gulkana has warmed, days and nights alike

Across 78 complete years: Gulkana's average low has risen +0.31°F per decade (95% range +0.11 to +0.50), about +2.5°F since 1943. Gulkana's average high has risen +0.35°F per decade (95% range +0.18 to +0.53), about +2.9°F since 1943.

How to read these
  • Trend: a straight line through every complete year. "Per decade" is how much it rises or falls every 10 years.
  • 95% range: how far the trend could plausibly be off, given how much years bounce around. If the whole range is above zero, the warming is real, not luck.
  • Signal, at the edge, noise: signal means the range clears zero; at the edge means it's at least 80% of the way there; noise means it doesn't come close.

5 · Rain and snow

Rain and snow: no clear long-term change
