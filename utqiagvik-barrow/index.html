3 · The record book

Record highs keep coming. Record lows have nearly stopped.

If Utqiagvik (Barrow)'s climate weren't changing, every year would have the same chance of holding a daily record. Since 2000, Utqiagvik (Barrow) has set 15 standing record lows, far fewer than the 92 that chance alone would give, and 146 record highs, far more than the 93 that chance alone would give. The 2010s hold the most record highs of any decade (66). Utqiagvik (Barrow)'s all-time high is 79°F, on July 13, 1993.

4 · How it's changed

Utqiagvik (Barrow) has warmed, days and nights alike

Across 102 complete years: Utqiagvik (Barrow)'s average low has risen +0.69°F per decade (95% range +0.53 to +0.85), about +7.2°F since 1921. Utqiagvik (Barrow)'s average high has risen +0.56°F per decade (95% range +0.39 to +0.74), about +5.9°F since 1921.

How to read these
  • Trend: a straight line through every complete year. "Per decade" is how much it rises or falls every 10 years.
  • 95% range: how far the trend could plausibly be off, given how much years bounce around. If the whole range is above zero, the warming is real, not luck.
  • Signal, at the edge, noise: signal means the range clears zero; at the edge means it's at least 80% of the way there; noise means it doesn't come close.

5 · Rain and snow

Utqiagvik (Barrow) has gotten wetter

Utqiagvik (Barrow)'s yearly precipitation has risen +0.24 inches per decade (95% range +0.13 to +0.36), about +2.5 inches since 1921. The 1991–2020 normal is 5.3 inches a year. The wettest year was 2019 (10.11 in); the driest, 1934 (1.00 in).
