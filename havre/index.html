3 · The record book

Havre is setting record highs far faster than chance

If Havre's climate weren't changing, every year would have the same chance of holding a daily record. Since 2000, Havre has set 61 standing record lows, close to the 67 that chance alone would give, and 102 record highs, far more than the 67 that chance alone would give. The 2000s hold the most record highs of any decade (42). Havre's all-time high is 111°F, on August 5, 1961.

4 · How it's changed

Havre's days got warmer. Its nights didn't.

Across 144 complete years: Havre's average low shows no clear change: +0.05°F per decade, a range (−0.02 to +0.13) that includes zero. Havre's average high has risen +0.29°F per decade (95% range +0.19 to +0.38), about +4.1°F since 1880.

How to read these
  • Trend: a straight line through every complete year. "Per decade" is how much it rises or falls every 10 years.
  • 95% range: how far the trend could plausibly be off, given how much years bounce around. If the whole range is above zero, the warming is real, not luck.
  • Signal, at the edge, noise: signal means the range clears zero; at the edge means it's at least 80% of the way there; noise means it doesn't come close.

5 · Rain and snow

Havre has gotten drier

Havre's yearly precipitation has fallen −0.21 inches per decade (95% range −0.34 to −0.08), about −3.1 inches since 1880. The 1991–2020 normal is 11.7 inches a year. The wettest year was 1884 (25.67 in); the driest, 1988 (6.38 in).
