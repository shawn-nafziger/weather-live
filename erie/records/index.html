What's the coldest temperature ever recorded in Erie?

The coldest temperature ever recorded in Erie is −18°F, on January 19, 1994, and February 16, 2015; the hottest is 100°F, on June 25, 1988. Its coldest day had a high of −4°F (January 19, 1994), and its warmest night never dropped below 82°F (August 7, 1918, and July 10, 1936).

  • Temperatures are whole degrees Fahrenheit, as NOAA records them. A record shared by several years lists every year. Records count every day through the latest final day, so a record set yesterday appears here today.
  • A date's record is shown only when at least 30 years have a value for it. Normals are 1991–2020 averages, smoothed over 15 days.
  • Unadjusted readings. This is the official city record as kept, without adjustments. It joins several observing sites over the years, and a move, a new instrument or the city growing around a station can shift readings (city growth mostly warms nights); NOAA's adjusted climate datasets correct for these. Treat any single number as approximate.
