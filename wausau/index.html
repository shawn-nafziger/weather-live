3 · The record book

Wausau has nearly stopped setting record lows

If Wausau's climate weren't changing, every year would have the same chance of holding a daily record. Since 2000, Wausau has set 23 standing record lows, far fewer than the 76 that chance alone would give, and 106 record highs, more than the 76 that chance alone would give. The 1930s hold the most record highs of any decade (60). Wausau's all-time high is 107°F, on July 13, 1936.

4 · How it's changed

Wausau has warmed, days and nights alike

Across 116 complete years: Wausau's average low has risen +0.23°F per decade (95% range +0.13 to +0.33), about +3.0°F since 1896. Wausau's average high has risen +0.14°F per decade (95% range +0.04 to +0.24), about +1.8°F since 1896.

How to read these
  • Trend: a straight line through every complete year. "Per decade" is how much it rises or falls every 10 years.
  • 95% range: how far the trend could plausibly be off, given how much years bounce around. If the whole range is above zero, the warming is real, not luck.
  • Signal, at the edge, noise: signal means the range clears zero; at the edge means it's at least 80% of the way there; noise means it doesn't come close.

5 · Rain and snow

Wausau has gotten wetter

Wausau's yearly precipitation has risen +0.60 inches per decade (95% range +0.30 to +0.90), about +7.7 inches since 1896. The 1991–2020 normal is 33.9 inches a year. The wettest year was 1938 (48.72 in); the driest, 1976 (21.43 in).
