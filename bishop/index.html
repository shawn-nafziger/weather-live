3 · The record book

Bishop is setting record highs far faster than chance

If Bishop's climate weren't changing, every year would have the same chance of holding a daily record. Since 2000, Bishop has set 86 standing record lows, fewer than the 118 that chance alone would give, and 184 record highs, far more than the 118 that chance alone would give. The 2020s hold the most record highs of any decade (92). Bishop's all-time high is 111°F, on July 10, 2021.

4 · How it's changed

Bishop has warmed, days and nights alike

Across 81 complete years: Bishop's average low has risen +0.23°F per decade (95% range +0.13 to +0.32), about +1.8°F since 1944. Bishop's average high has risen +0.24°F per decade (95% range +0.10 to +0.38), about +2.0°F since 1944.

How to read these
  • Trend: a straight line through every complete year. "Per decade" is how much it rises or falls every 10 years.
  • 95% range: how far the trend could plausibly be off, given how much years bounce around. If the whole range is above zero, the warming is real, not luck.
  • Signal, at the edge, noise: signal means the range clears zero; at the edge means it's at least 80% of the way there; noise means it doesn't come close.
