How to read these records

  • Temperatures are whole degrees Fahrenheit, as NOAA records them. A record shared by several years lists every year. Records count every day through the latest final day, so a record set yesterday appears here today.
  • A date's record is shown only when at least 30 years have a value for it. Normals are 1991–2020 averages, smoothed over 15 days.
  • Unadjusted readings. {{CAVEAT}}
