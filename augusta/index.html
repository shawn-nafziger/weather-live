3 · The record book

Augusta is setting record highs far faster than chance

If Augusta's climate weren't changing, every year would have the same chance of holding a daily record. Since 2000, Augusta has set 74 standing record lows, close to the 64 that chance alone would give, and 121 record highs, far more than the 64 that chance alone would give. The 2010s hold the most record highs of any decade (69). Augusta's all-time high is 108°F, on August 21, 1983.

4 · How it's changed

Augusta's nights cooled over the long run but have warmed since 1986. Its days have warmed.

Across 151 complete years: Augusta's average low has fallen −0.28°F per decade (95% range −0.34 to −0.22), about −4.2°F since 1875. Augusta's average high has risen +0.22°F per decade (95% range +0.17 to +0.26), about +3.2°F since 1875. The last 40 years tell a different story: since 1986, lows have risen +0.49°F per decade (95% range +0.11 to +0.88) and highs have risen +0.47°F per decade (95% range +0.09 to +0.85). The full record joins different observing sites over the years, and a move (for example, from the city to a cooler airport) can hide or exaggerate a long-run trend, so the recent decades are the clearer guide here.

How to read these
  • Trend: a straight line through every complete year. "Per decade" is how much it rises or falls every 10 years.
  • 95% range: how far the trend could plausibly be off, given how much years bounce around. If the whole range is above zero, the warming is real, not luck.
  • Signal, at the edge, noise: signal means the range clears zero; at the edge means it's at least 80% of the way there; noise means it doesn't come close.

5 · Rain and snow

Rain: no clear long-term change

Augusta's yearly precipitation shows no clear long-term change (−0.13 inches per decade, a range that includes zero). The 1991–2020 normal is 44.1 inches a year. The wettest year was 1929 (73.82 in); the driest, 1933 (28.05 in). Augusta almost never sees snow (the 1991–2020 average is 0.8 inches a winter), so there's no snowfall chart.
